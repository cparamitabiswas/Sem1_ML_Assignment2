# Dataset Source

**Predict Students' Dropout and Academic Success**
UCI Machine Learning Repository, dataset id 697.
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/697/predict+students+dropout+and+academic+success

Originally compiled by Realinho, V., Vieira Martins, M., Machado, J., and
Baptista, L. from a higher education institution, covering students enrolled
across several undergraduate degrees (agronomy, design, education, nursing,
journalism, management, social service, and technologies). Records combine
data known at enrollment (demographics, socio-economic factors, academic
path) with academic performance at the end of the first and second
semesters.

Licensed for reuse under CC BY 4.0.

`dataset.csv` in this folder is the dataset exactly as published, with only
the column headers stripped of stray whitespace (no rows/values changed).
